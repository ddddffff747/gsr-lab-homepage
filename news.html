Conference2026. 11. 04–06

Paper presented at PBD-V in Chile

Lee DongYeon (Ph.D. student) presented a paper at the 5th International Conference on Performance-Based Design in Earthquake Geotechnical Engineering (PBD-V), held at the Hotel Dreams de los Volcanes in Puerto Varas, Chile, on November 4–6, 2026. The conference is organized by the Chilean Society of Geotechnical Engineering (SOCHIGE) together with the International Society for Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering (ISSMGE).

  • Feature-Level Motion Modulation Conditioned on Site Profiles for Seismic Amplification Prediction

After the conference the group visited the Osorno Volcano in the Los Lagos Region of southern Chile.

Project2026. 07

New NRF project MetaEX launched

The laboratory began MetaEX — Meta Cognitive AI Laboratory for Autonomous Design of Underground EXcavation (2026–2029), funded by the National Research Foundation of Korea. The project develops an autonomous analysis and design platform for deep underground excavation, integrating open-source finite element analysis with physics-informed artificial intelligence and meta-cognitive decision-making toward a Level 4 digital twin.
