Project2026. 07

New NRF project MetaEX launched

The laboratory began MetaEX — Meta Cognitive AI Laboratory for Autonomous Design of Underground EXcavation (2026–2029), funded by the National Research Foundation of Korea. The project develops an autonomous analysis and design platform for deep underground excavation, integrating open-source finite element analysis with physics-informed artificial intelligence and meta-cognitive decision-making toward a Level 4 digital twin. Conference2026. 06. 14–19

Two papers presented at the 21st ICSMGE in Vienna

Members of the laboratory presented two papers at the 21st International Conference on Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering (ICSMGE), held at the Austria Center Vienna, Austria, on June 14–19, 2026. The conference marked the 100th anniversary of modern soil mechanics under the slogan “Where it all began.”

  • Lee DongYeon (Ph.D. student) — Numerical estimation of lagged coherency function at a hard rock site in Korea
  • Lee Yonggook (Post-doctoral researcher) — Seismic Earth Pressure on Basement Walls Underlain by Bedrock: A Numerical Study
